# Products — Access Rules

## Organization User Role

Role yang melekat pada `OrganizationUser`:

- `OWNER`
- `ADMIN`
- `MEMBER`

Role ini hanya berlaku dalam organization terkait.

## Permissions

| Action          | OWNER | ADMIN | MEMBER |
| --------------- | :---: | :---: | :----: |
| View Purchase   |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Create Purchase |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Edit Purchase   |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Delete Purchase |  ✅   |  ❌   |   ❌   |
